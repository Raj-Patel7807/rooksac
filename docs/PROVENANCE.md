## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Critical Provenance Items

### 2.1 Piece-Square Tables (PeSTO / CPW)
- **Origin**: Tomasz Michniewski published the simplified piece-square tables for PeSTO on the Chess Programming Wiki. They are universally cited as public educational reference tables for classical evaluation baselines.
- **Project Usage**: Initialized in `engine/src/evaluation.cpp` within `initDefaultParameters()`.
- **Policy**:
  - The tables are not copied executable source code; they are numerical constants.
  - Their presence is explicitly credited in source comments and notices.
  - RookSac includes a Texel tuning framework (`engine/src/tuning.cpp`) capable of re-tuning these constants against custom game data if required by tournament rules.

### 2.2 Neural Evaluator & PyTorch Research Pipeline
- **Claim vs Reality**:
  - `docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md` references a native neural evaluator.
  - Inspection of `engine/src/neural_evaluator.cpp` reveals `loadModel()` does not load external weights, and `evaluate()` passes classical eval through a sigmoid transformation.
  - **Verdict**: The C++ neural evaluator is currently a placeholder scaffold. No unverified or third-party neural weights exist in the engine binary. The active production evaluation is 100% classical tapered evaluation.

### 2.3 Training Data Policy for Offline Research (Sprint 08)
- **Lichess Database License & Terms**:
  - Games on `database.lichess.org` are dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0.
  - Competition regulations (e.g. TCEC, CCC) permit models trained on Lichess CC0 game records provided no copyrighted proprietary network weights or proprietary engine evaluations are ingested.
- **Data Hygiene Rules**:
  - Raw PGN archives (which can be 30GB+ compressed) must **never** be committed to the git repository.
  - Only compact, deterministic dataset manifests and training metadata JSONs (`research/data/*_manifest.json`, `data_quality_report.json`) are committed.
  - All data ingestion occurs through streaming generator pipelines (`tools/pgn_extract.py`) to bound peak working memory.
  - Whole-game partitioning strictly prevents position-level data leakage across train, validation, and test splits.
